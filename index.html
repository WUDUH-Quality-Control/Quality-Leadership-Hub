Quality Leadership Hub

One file that carries six independent tools. Pick a tool on the left — each keeps its own upload flow and runs entirely in your browser, so no workbook ever leaves this page. The theme toggle in the top bar applies across every tool.

1

Seniors Tasks Tracker

The recurring-task calendar for the four Quality Seniors — Wakeel, Shadi, Jana and Enmanuel — covering every assignment from May to December 2026. Each row carries its task, owner, due date, cadence (weekly, bi-daily, twice-a-week, monthly, or a named business day) and any reference sheet or video. Three views: Table for sorting and searching the full list, Cards for a scannable feed, and By senior for four side-by-side columns showing who owns what. Filter by senior, task type or month, and today’s row is highlighted automatically. Built by Hamza Mehyar; the data is embedded, so nothing needs uploading.

2

Assignment Distributor

Routes every active agent to a team and distributes audits by AHT-weighted workload against real analyst capacity, honouring coaching conflicts and editable department/division eligibility rules. Weekly distribute generates a fresh book; Daily rebalance takes yesterday's export and equalises remaining work across each team, inheriting the roster, exceptions and holidays from that file.

3

Payments Auto Distribution

fxPick5 sampling with round-robin analyst assignment. Four views — Agent List (the final output), Notes, Tasks, and the Distribution query builder.

4

QC Audits Merger

Merges audit exports across 23 SharePoint list types with source auto-detection and date & week filtering into one clean sheet.

5

Weekly Business Report

Upload the Master Sheet once and build both Call and Non-Call reports for the previous completed week. A Classic WBR section reproduces the standard tables, plus an Enhanced Insights layer of trend, risk, momentum, Pareto and coverage charts.

6

Resource Allocation

Reads the NPT and Leaves SharePoint exports and turns them into a management view of productive-time loss — NPT % per analyst, where the time goes by category, leave patterns, and true productive availability (scheduled − leave − NPT) on a daily, weekly, or monthly grain.

Each tool is embedded in its own isolated frame, so their upload flows and internal state never collide. Switching tools keeps whatever you had loaded in the others intact for this session.
